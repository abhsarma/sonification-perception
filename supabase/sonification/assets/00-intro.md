# A perceptual study of different data sonification encodings

Thank you for choosing to participate!

In this study, you will be shown a sonification of a chart (the values in the chart are mapped to an auditory channel such as pitch or loudness). You will have to answer questions based on the charts that you hear. The study consists of 65 trials, excluding any attention checks. You can also review your task again using the *help* button on the top right corner of the page. Please read these instructions carefully.

You can quit at any point, but be aware that you cannot resume once this window is closed, and nor will you be compensated. After you have finished all the trials, you will be redirected back to Prolific.
